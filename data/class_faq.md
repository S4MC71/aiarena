# Arena Web Security Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Class Routine

## 1. Class Routine & Schedule
- **Live Class Days**: Every **Tuesday** and **Friday**.
- **Class Time**: **9:00 PM** (Bangladesh Standard Time, GMT+6).
- **Platform**: Live on Zoom. Joining links are posted on the secret Discord channel 30 minutes before every class.

## 2. Class Recordings & Materials
- **When are recordings uploaded?** Within 6 hours of class completion, full 1080p HD recordings with downloadable slides and lab notes are posted on the Student Portal.
- **Validity**: Lifetime access to all recorded lectures and course updates.

## 3. Mentor Office Hours & Support
- **Support Days**: Every **Sunday from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM**.
- **Where**: Discord Voice Channel (1-on-1 screenshare and doubt clearing with lead instructors).
- Students can also post questions anytime in the `#lab-help` Discord channel for assistance within 2 hours.

## 4. Certification & Exam Criteria
- Students must achieve at least a **70% score** in the 48-Hour Capstone CTF Exam.
- Upon passing, a verifiable digital certificate with a unique credential ID and LinkedIn badge is issued within 3 working days.
